# Human-Aware Multi-Robot Perception for Adaptive Warehouse Operations

## Solution

Builds a human-aware multi-robot perception and coordination system for adaptive warehouse operations using Smorphy robots. Combining human intent recognition, real-time environment perception, and dynamic task allocation.

## Sub-Solutions

- **Human-Aware** — A system's ability to perceive, respect, and adapt to human presence, intentions, comfort, and social norms, rather than treating people as static obstacles.
- **Multi-Robot** — A network of multiple robots (a Multi-Robot System, MRS) operating in the same environment, interacting, communicating, and coordinating to achieve common or distributed goals.
  - **Perception** — The collective process by which a team of robots senses, interprets, and understands their shared physical environment.
- **Coordination System** — The framework of software algorithms, communication protocols, and control architectures that enables multiple automated machines or robots to work together without conflict.
- **Adaptive Warehouse Operations** — A logistics framework where warehouse management systems, software, and autonomous robots automatically change their behavior in real-time to respond to sudden, unpredictable disruptions or shifts in demand.

## Areas Addressed by the Solution

Enabling robots to adjust workflows, prioritize human requests, and improve throughput and safety in real-time through collaborative decision-making and adaptive scheduling.

- **Prioritize Human Requests** — Any human command, intervention, or safety signal takes absolute precedence over autonomous operations; the system must instantly pause, override, or alter its current behavior to execute human intent safely and without delay.
- **Improve Throughput and Safety in Real-Time** — The system must maximize productive output (throughput) while simultaneously maintaining accident prevention (safety) under fluid, instantly changing conditions, dynamically optimizing both at the moment events occur.
- **Collaborative Decision-Making** — A decentralized process where multiple autonomous agents share local data, negotiate options, and reach a unified agreement on a course of action rather than relying on a single leader.
- **Enable Robots to Adjust Workflows** — Autonomous machines alter their sequences, tasks, and operational paths in response to shifting conditions, evaluating live data to change what, when, and how work is completed.
